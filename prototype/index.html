Simulations

Simulate 1,000 pulls runs this exact raid against 1,000 different seeds. Compare tactic cards runs 250 pulls per card. Play a 10-week season starts these ten as a brand-new guild and raids 5 pulls a week, 20 times over, so you can watch them get better together.

Targets are for a raid a few weeks in. Heroic: unprepared under 10%, partly prepared 20-40%, fully prepared 40-60%, min-maxed 70%+. Normal (fresh guild): about 70% unprepared. Mythic: 30-40% min-maxed. Veterans add about 15 points on fully prepared Heroic. Ragnaroast's right card is Burn.

Prototype, engine version 1. Traits, morale, loot and the other bosses are not in yet. Skill (0-100), boss familiarity (0-10) and chemistry between pairs (0-20) each add up to about +2% power; skill also dodges Magma Splash, familiarity cuts mechanic damage and a tank's bond with a healer boosts that healer's heals on them. Melee specs outside the Melee zone, and tanks outside Front, hit at half damage. Ragnaroast's throw, splash and adds are fire: fire resistance from gear and a paladin aura or shaman totem cuts it, up to 60%. Values for food, elixirs, flasks, crit and Ragnaroast's damage are Tuning's first guesses, set in the engine's balance data. Each fight's checksum is identical in this browser and in Lua 5.1, the version WoW runs.
