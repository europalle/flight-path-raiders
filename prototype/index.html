1,000 pulls

Simulate 1,000 pulls runs this exact raid against 1,000 different seeds. Compare tactic cards runs 250 pulls per card.

Tuning targets with a sensible raid: about 70% kills on Normal, 40-60% on Heroic with the right card, 15-30% on Mythic. Ragnaroast's right card is Burn: race him before the throws add up.

Prototype, engine version 1. Traits, morale, loot and the other bosses are not in yet. Melee specs outside the Melee zone, and tanks outside Front, hit at half damage. Values for food, elixirs, flasks, crit and Ragnaroast's damage are Tuning's first guesses, set in the engine's balance data. Each fight's checksum is identical in this browser and in Lua 5.1, the version WoW runs.
