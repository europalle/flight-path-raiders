# Gemini prompt pack v4: copy-ready

Art department, 2026-11-18. Every prompt below is complete. Copy the whole grey box into Gemini as it is, attach the image listed, and send me the PNG it makes.

- **Always attach** `art/sketches/character-study-v1.png` (the character study). Character prompts E1 to E10 also need your E0 result.
- **Run them in order**, one per message. Batches of results are fine.
- **Save as PNG** when Gemini offers it; JPEG leaves pink fringes around cut-out edges.
- **Rerun** anything that looks like Blizzard's real art, logos or characters. Look-alike check before Art accepts a piece (gate review, 2026-11-18): no named character's signature features (antlers plus green hair on a night elf druid, a specific raid set's wings and gold), no Blizzard faction crests, no copied UI frame shapes. If two of us think "that's X", it's rerun.
- **v4 (gate review):** prompts no longer name the game. They describe the traits instead: chunky hand-painted fantasy, big shoulder pads, gold-trimmed frames. Our parody stays obvious without leaning on someone else's name.
- **Why the magenta and the greys:** Art cuts out the magenta, and grey pieces get recoloured in the game, so one drawing serves both light and dark mode. The reuse plan is at the end.
