# UX/UI & Accessibility

Screen flows, wireframes and accessibility standards. Wireframes here are approved before Art paints over them.

## Standards

- **Glance test:** every decision screen is readable in 5 seconds; at most three numbers per card.
- **Never colour alone:** quality, morale and affixes always carry a shape, icon or text as well. Colour-blind palettes are a setting.
- **Contrast and size:** text stays legible at WoW's lowest UI scale, on both light and dark board themes.
- **Motion:** combat playback can be sped up, skipped, or shown as a static summary.
- **Click budget:** prepping a boss takes a handful of clicks, with sensible defaults such as "same as last pull".
- **Interruption-safe:** every screen pauses and resumes on landing without losing anything.
- **Ready for translation:** layouts leave room for longer text.
