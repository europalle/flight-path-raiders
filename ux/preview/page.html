Flight Path Raiders: board preview

The board on your own 1440p screen, at its real in-game size. Click the board's tabs and buttons to move between screens, drag it by any empty spot, change the scale like /fpr scale, and switch between the light and dark theme. Esc closes it, like in game.

The board is closed. Type /fpr (the button above) to open it.

What you're looking at

  • Hub is hi-fi, built from your Gemini kit, in both themes. Gold, DKP and rank have icons; contrast is measured on every line.
  • Every other screen is the approved mid-fi wireframe at the same size, so you can judge layout, reading distance and how much of the game the board covers. Hi-fi for them is issue #32; the Journal and loot window still need wireframes (#31).
  • Default size is 70% of the screen width; the scale goes from 70% to 120% of that, like /fpr scale.

Try this flow

  • Hub: Prep pull, then Pull, then Skip to result, then Back to hub or Next boss.
  • Hub tabs: Roster, Tavern, Guild Hall, Bank. The cog opens Settings; Done brings you back.
  • On any screen, the title (Flight Path Raiders) takes you back to the hub. The title bar's minus and plus change the scale; the X closes the board.
  • Look at 70% and at 120%: is the text readable at 70%? Does 120% cover too much of the game?
