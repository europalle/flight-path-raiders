# Phase 3 survey: the fixed questions (gate check 1)

QA (Player Research), 2026-11-19. Issue #34. Fill this in **after one full in-game week** with the addon installed: from one weekly reset to the next, playing whenever you'd normally fly or wait. The questions are fixed: don't change them between builds, so later answers can be compared with these.

## How it's scored

Each scored question is 1 to 5:
1 = strongly disagree, 2 = disagree, 3 = not sure, 4 = agree, 5 = strongly agree.

**Gate check 1 passes** when questions 1, 2 and 3 all score **4 or 5**. Any 1-3 on those three is a fail, and the free-text answers say what to fix. Questions 4-10 don't decide the gate. They point at what to work on next.
