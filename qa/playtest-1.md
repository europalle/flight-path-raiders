# Playtest 1: the Molten Bore, solo

Written by QA (Player Research) for the Director. Build: playtest page, engine v7.
About 60-90 minutes over one or two sittings. Your save stays in the browser.

## What we want to learn

1. Does a week feel like a raid week? Prep, pull, loot, try again.
2. Does the learning path work? Do you work out each boss before the recap tells you?
3. Is Heroic a test you want to retake, or a wall?
4. Do skill, familiarity and chemistry feel like anything, or are they invisible?

## Session 1: week 1, Normal (20-30 minutes)

- Don't open the Journal or read the boss notes first. Pick your 10 and pull Magmadad.
- Clear as far as you can on Normal. Pull again as often as you like.
- Run your dungeons and hand out loot when you feel like it.
- Write down, per boss: how many pulls, and the moment you knew what to do (your own idea, a clue, or the answer).

## Session 2: weeks 2-4, Heroic opens (20-30 minutes)

- Once Ragnaroast dies on Normal, Heroic opens. One attempt per boss per week.
- Try at least one Heroic pull with no cauldrons, so you feel the difference.
- When someone gets benched, notice whether you change the plan or just wait for reset.
- Try "Replay that pull" at least once after a wipe.

## Session 3: weeks 5-10, if you want more (20-30 minutes)

- Push for Heroic Ragnaroast. Buy Fire Whelp gear only if you've worked out why.
- Hire someone from the Tavern and put them in the raid. Did you notice they're worse at first?

## Questions after each session

- What was the most fun moment? The most annoying one?
- Did any wipe feel unfair? Which one, and why?
- Did a recap ever tell you something you'd already worked out? Did one come too late?
- Was there a screen where you didn't know what to do next?
- Did you look at the fight log, or just the result?
- What would you want before your next raid night?

Paste your notes into the chat, or just tell us. Tuning's bot results for comparison are in
`tools/playtest_bot.js` (a bot that only follows the recaps clears Heroic Ragnaroast around week 7-8).
