# Positioning and addon page plan (Marketing and Community)

Issue #13, 2026-11-19. The 30-second clip is `marketing/clip-pull.mp4`. For now it is drawn by the addon's own code outside the game (tools/addon_clip.py); it gets recaptured in the client at install.

## One line

**Run a raid guild while you fly.** A raid auto-battler inside WoW: Forever. Pick ten raiders, plan the pull, watch it play out, hand out the loot, and come back next week smarter.

## Who it's for

- Players on long flight paths, waiting for a queue, or in the dead time between pulls in their real raid.
- Raid leaders who enjoy the planning more than the button-pressing.
- People who like the old raid jokes: the bomb running into the healers, the curse nobody decursed, "who brought fire resistance?"

## What makes it different

1. **It's a parody with love.** Magmadad, Lucifrown and Ragnaroast are our own jokes about raid nights everyone remembers.
2. **You learn by wiping.** The game tells you what went wrong in the raid leader's words and only gives you the answer once you've earned it.
3. **Your guild has people in it.** Traits like Needs on Everything, Alt-Tabbed and Pet on Aggressive, and morale that makes them quit.
4. **Same raid, fair race.** Every guild fights the same seeded fights, so scores can be compared and replays checked (leaderboards come in Phase 4).

## Discord and release notes

- One Discord server with: #announcements, #raid-stories (players post their best wipes), #bugs, #ideas.
- Release notes in plain words: what changed, what to try, and known issues. Every note links the replay checksum version, so people know when old replays stop verifying.
- First posts: the clip, then one boss a day with its pull line.
